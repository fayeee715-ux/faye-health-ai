Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 12, 2026

1. Overview

Faye Health AI is a personal wellness analysis application currently in development. It is intended for use by its owner.

2. Data We Plan to Collect

With authorization, the application may access sleep, readiness, heart rate, activity, workout, and related wellness data from the Oura API.

3. How Data Will Be Used

Authorized data will be used to generate personal wellness summaries, analyze trends, and provide general lifestyle insights.

4. Data Processing

The planned system may use secure cloud infrastructure and an AI service provider to process authorized health information. Processing details will be updated before live health data is connected.

5. Data Sharing

Health data will not be published on this public website. Any transfer to a service provider will be limited to the purposes described in this policy.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

Retention periods, deletion procedures, and storage safeguards will be documented before production use.

7. Contact

For privacy questions, contact the application owner using the contact email provided in the Oura developer application.
